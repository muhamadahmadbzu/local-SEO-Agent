# Website rental / lead agreement — term sheet outline

> Outline for discussion with a lawyer licensed in your state. It is **not** a contract and **not** legal advice.

1. **Parties**: <Operator LLC> ("Owner") and <Tenant legal name>, license #<...> ("Client").
2. **Asset**: Owner owns the website <domain>, its content, and the tracking phone number(s). Client gets the
   exclusive right to receive leads for the **Services** in the **Territory** during the Term. No transfer of
   ownership.
3. **Services and territory**: <services list>; <cities / ZIPs>.
4. **Fees**: <flat $X/month in advance> OR <$Y per Qualified Lead, billed weekly/monthly, cap $Z> OR <base + per lead>.
   Late payment: Owner may pause routing after <N> days past due.
5. **Qualified lead definition** (per-lead deals): an inbound call of ≥ <60-90> seconds or a form submission from
   within the Territory about the Services, unique by phone number within 30 days, excluding spam, solicitors,
   wrong numbers and existing customers. Disputes are resolved with call recordings within <7> days.
6. **Term**: <trial>, then an initial term of <3> months, renewing month to month. Either party may terminate on 30
   days' written notice. Owner may terminate immediately for license lapse, consumer complaints or non-payment.
7. **Client obligations**: hold all required licenses and insurance (provide certificates); answer or return calls
   promptly; serve customers lawfully; supply true business details (name, license, photos, claims) for the site;
   never ask Owner to publish claims Client can't substantiate.
8. **Owner obligations**: operate and maintain the site and number; disclose call recording; provide monthly
   reports; keep site claims truthful and approved by Client.
9. **No guarantees**: Owner doesn't guarantee rankings, call volume or revenue.
10. **Branding and GBP**: the site may display Client's name, license and contact details while active. Client's
    Google Business Profile, reviews and customer data remain Client's. On termination, Owner removes Client
    details within <X> days.
11. **Data and privacy**: call recordings and lead data are used only to deliver and verify leads, under the site's
    privacy policy. No resale of Client's customer data.
12. **Liability**: each party is responsible for its own acts. Client indemnifies Owner for claims arising from
    Client's work. Liability caps: <...>.
13. **Governing law and venue**: <state>.
