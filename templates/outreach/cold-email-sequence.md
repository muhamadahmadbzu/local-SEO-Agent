# Cold email sequence — tenant prospects (3 touches, plain text)

Rules: short, specific, true. No fake urgency, no exaggerated numbers. Include an opt-out line. Send from a real
domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set. Commercial email must follow CAN-SPAM (accurate header and subject, a physical
postal address, opt-out honored within 10 business days).

---

**Email 1: day 0**
Subject: <service> calls in <town>

Hi <first name>,

I run a website that ranks for "<core keyword>" in <town>. Last month it brought in <N> calls from homeowners
asking about <service 1> and <service 2>.

I don't do the work, so I'm looking for one reliable <service> company to send these calls to. Would you like
to try it free for <7/14> days? If the jobs are good, we can talk about keeping it going.

<your name> · <phone>
<postal address> · Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.

---

**Email 2: day 3**
Subject: Re: <service> calls in <town>

<first name>, a quick follow-up. Here's a typical call from last week (recorded with disclosure): <link to a short
anonymized clip or a call summary: "Homeowner in <neighborhood>, 2 oak trees over the roof, wants quotes this week">.

Want me to route the next ones to you for a few days?

---

**Email 3: day 8**
Subject: Closing the loop

<first name>, I'll offer the trial to another <service> company in <town> this week unless you want it. Either way,
no problem. Just reply "yes" and I'll set it up today.
