# Cold call script — tenant prospect

Goal: get the owner to agree to a **free trial** of calls. Don't sell SEO. Sell customers.
Before calling: you need real call volume (or a live inbound call you can transfer), the trial terms, and the
prospect's details (services, area, license status, reviews). Be honest. Never invent volume.

---

**Opener (10 seconds)**
"Hi, is this <owner name>? This is <your name>. I'll be quick. I run a local website for <service> in <town>. It gets
calls from people who need <service> and I don't do the work myself. I'm looking for one company to send them to.
Are you taking on new <service> jobs right now?"

- *Yes* -> Proof
- *Busy* -> "Totally get it. When's a better 2 minutes, later today or tomorrow morning?"

**Proof (30 seconds)**
"Last month the site got <N> calls from people in <towns> asking about <top services>. I can forward them to you
for <7/14> days free, so you see the quality yourself. If they're good jobs, we talk about keeping them coming. If
not, no hard feelings."

**Qualify (ask, then listen)**
- "Which areas do you cover?" / "Which jobs do you want more of, and which do you not want?"
- "How quickly do you answer the phone during the day? After hours?"
- "Are you licensed and insured for this work in <state>?" (needed before routing customers)
- "Are you spending on Google Ads, LSAs or Angi right now? Roughly what does a customer cost you?"

**Close the trial**
"Here's what I'd suggest: I point the number to your cell starting <date> for <7/14> days. You'll hear a short
message before each call saying it's from the <brand> website, so you know where it came from. At the end we look
at the calls together. Sound fair?"

**Objections**
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I have enough work" | "Makes sense. Is that true in <slow months> too? Many owners use this to fill the slow season or to get more of the high-ticket jobs." |
| "SEO never worked for me" | "This isn't SEO you pay for and hope. The site already ranks. You only keep it if the calls are worth it." |
| "How much?" | "Let's see if the calls are good first. For context, it'll cost less per customer than Google Ads in <town>, usually around <$range> a month." |
| "Is it exclusive?" | "Yes. One company per service area. That's the point." |
| "Send me info" | "Happy to. What I'd rather do is send you two real calls this week. Info sheets don't fix a schedule." |

**After the call**: log it in `ops/tenant-pipeline.md` (status, notes, next step, date).
