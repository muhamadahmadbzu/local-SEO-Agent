## Non-negotiables (house rules, apply to every project)

- No fake Google Business Profiles, virtual-office or borrowed addresses. Lead generators are ineligible for a
  GBP. Map-pack presence comes later, from the tenant's real profile.
- No fake reviews, testimonials, ratings, awards, licenses, years in business, team members or project photos.
- While unrented, the site presents itself honestly as a referral/matching service and never as the contractor.
- Recorded calls get a recording disclosure. Forms carry clear consent language. No outbound robocalls or texts
  without prior express written consent.
- No regulated vertical (legal, insurance, medical, finance, addiction treatment) without a compliance memo that
  clears it.
- Content is useful, specific and locally true. No templated city pages that only swap the city name.
