## How it works

1. **Call or send a request.** Tell us what the tree is, where it stands and what you want done.
2. **Get connected.** Your request goes to an independent local provider who serves your area.
3. **Get an on-site estimate.** Tree work can't be quoted honestly from a photo alone. Access, size and what the tree could fall on all change the job.
4. **Decide.** Compare the scope, price, insurance documents and schedule before you agree to anything.

## What affects the price of tree work

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Height and trunk diameter | Bigger trees need more climbing, rigging and haul-away time. |
| Location | Trees over houses, power lines or fences need careful rigging and sometimes a crane. |
| Tree condition | Dead or storm-split wood is unpredictable and takes slower, more careful work. |
| Access | Backyards without equipment access mean more hand-carrying. |
| Cleanup | Hauling wood, chipping brush and grinding the stump are often priced separately. |

For typical price ranges, read the [tree removal cost guide](/cost/tree-removal-cost/).

## Questions to ask any tree service

- Can you show a **certificate of liability insurance**, and do you carry workers' compensation for your crew?
- Who handles **utility lines**? Only utility-approved line-clearance crews should work near energized lines. Call the utility first.
- Does the price include **cleanup, haul-away and stump grinding**?
- Will you need a **permit**? Some cities regulate removing trees in the public right-of-way. Check with your city before work starts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Demo City?

Rules vary by city and by where the tree stands. Trees between the sidewalk and the street often belong to the city. Call your city's public works or forestry office before you remove a tree near the street.

### Is storm damage covered by homeowners insurance?

It depends on your policy and on what the tree hit. Insurers often cover removal when a tree falls on a covered structure. Call your insurance company. A tree service can document the damage but cannot decide what is covered.

### How fast can someone come out?

That depends on the provider's schedule and on storm demand. After a major storm, hazardous trees on homes and driveways usually get priority.
