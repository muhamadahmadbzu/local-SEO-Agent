Demo City Tree Service is a free referral service. When you call the number on this site or send a request, we connect you with an independent local tree care provider who serves your area.

## What we are and what we aren't

- We **are** a way to reach a local tree service quickly, and a place for clear, practical information about tree work in Demo City.
- We **are not** a tree care company. We don't employ crews, set prices or perform services. The provider you speak with is an independent business, responsible for its own work, licensing and insurance.

## How we write our guides

Our pages explain how tree work is done and what drives the price, so you can ask better questions. Where we mention rules, such as permits or utility lines, we point you to the authority that decides. Always confirm with them.
