Oak Hills was built into existing woods, so many homes sit under mature oaks that were there long before the houses. That's the appeal, and it's also the challenge. Slopes complicate equipment access, and oaks respond badly to the root damage that construction and grading cause.

## What's different about tree work here

- **Slope access**: steep driveways and back slopes often rule out bucket trucks, so climbers and rigging do more of the work.
- **Oak root zones**: trenching, new patios and soil piled over roots can kill an oak years later. Plan hardscape work around the drip line.
- **HOA review**: some subdivisions require approval before removing trees over a certain size. Check your covenants first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the best time to prune oaks?

Many arborists avoid pruning oaks during the warm months when sap beetles are active, to reduce the risk of spreading oak wilt where it occurs. Dormant-season pruning is the usual recommendation. Ask the provider about local guidance.

### My HOA requires approval. What do I need?

Usually photos of the tree, the reason for removal and sometimes a written assessment. Ask the provider for a short written assessment you can submit.
