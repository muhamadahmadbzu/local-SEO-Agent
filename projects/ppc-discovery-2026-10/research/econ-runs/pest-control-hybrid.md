# Lead economics - Pest Control

## Assumptions

| Input | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Average ticket | $424 | niche prior (geo-mean of [150, 1200]) |
| Tenant close rate | 35% | default for mixed niches |
| Tenant gross margin | 40% | user/default |
| Median CPC | n/a | user |
| Pay-per-call payout | $48 | niche prior midpoint |
| Calls share / billable share | 80% / 50% | default |
| Months to full ranking | 9 | assumed |
| Build cost / monthly cost / links | $60 / $15 / $0 x 6 mo | default |

## What a lead is worth

- Revenue per lead to the tenant: **$148** (ticket x close rate)
- Gross profit per lead to the tenant: **$59**
- Fair price per exclusive lead: **$15 - $27**
- Tenant's Google Ads cost per lead: **n/a** (CPC / 10% conversion)
- Niche market priors: rent [500, 1500] / mo, per lead [30, 80], pay-per-call [25, 70]
