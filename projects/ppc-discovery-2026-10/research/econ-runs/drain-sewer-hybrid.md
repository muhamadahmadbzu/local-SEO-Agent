## What a lead is worth

- Revenue per lead to the tenant: **$671** (ticket x close rate)
- Gross profit per lead to the tenant: **$268**
- Fair price per exclusive lead: **$54 - $121**
- Tenant's Google Ads cost per lead: **n/a** (CPC / 10% conversion)
- Niche market priors: rent [600, 1800] / mo, per lead [30, 100], pay-per-call [25, 70]
