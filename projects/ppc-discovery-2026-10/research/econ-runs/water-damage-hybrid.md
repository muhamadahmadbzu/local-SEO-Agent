- Gross profit per lead to the tenant: **$775**
- Fair price per exclusive lead: **$155 - $349**
- Tenant's Google Ads cost per lead: **n/a** (CPC / 10% conversion)
- Niche market priors: rent [1500, 3500] / mo, per lead [75, 250], pay-per-call [50, 200]
