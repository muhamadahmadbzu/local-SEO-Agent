- Fair price per exclusive lead: **$490 - $1,102**

- Niche market priors: rent [800, 2500] / mo, per lead [100, 300], pay-per-call [50, 200]

## Notes

- Leads supplied manually (state where they came from in the memo).
- Target rent capped at the niche's market prior high ($2,500). Value-based pricing above market needs proof (call recordings, closed-job data) before you ask for it.
- Every value-per-lead input should come from the tenant conversation (ticket, close rate) once one exists.
- Kill criteria belong in the IC decision: e.g. 'fewer than N leads/mo by month M -> pivot or sell calls only'.
