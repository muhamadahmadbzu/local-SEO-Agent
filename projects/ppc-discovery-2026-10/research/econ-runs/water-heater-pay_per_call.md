## What a lead is worth

- Revenue per lead to the tenant: **$581** (ticket x close rate)
- Gross profit per lead to the tenant: **$232**
- Fair price per exclusive lead: **$46 - $105**
- Tenant's Google Ads cost per lead: **n/a** (CPC / 10% conversion)
- Niche market priors: rent [500, 1500] / mo, per lead [30, 90], pay-per-call [25, 70]
