## Steelman (the strongest honest case FOR)
**Overall:** Gate 1 asks which niches deserve the next $0 of research, not which site to build. Both memos agree on the main risk: offer access and SEO-traffic acceptance are UNKNOWN. Both also propose a $0 test (apply to networks) before any spending. Cash at risk is about $90-250 [ASSUMPTION], so a wrong pick costs little money. The real cost is operator time.
- **Tree service:** it has the most distinct public pay-per-call listings (3 OfferVault snippets with a 90s buffer) [OBSERVED-snippet]. Its priors show rankability 6, risk 9.4 and R&R score #2 [PRIOR], so it has a credible fallback to a tenant if pay-per-call stalls.
- **Water damage:** it has the highest payout per call and is the only niche where a snippet names SEO as an allowed traffic source (E-5) [OBSERVED-snippet]. It needs the fewest calls to break even, and demand is fairly flat across the year [PRIOR].
- **Pest control:** it has many listings and high demand [PRIOR], and the season is long in the South.
- **Septic:** it has the best rankability (9) and the best R&R prior (#1). If a buyer exists, it is the niche most likely to rank on a $0 link budget.
- **HVAC:** it has huge demand, many networks buy it, and calls are urgent [PRIOR].
- **Drain/sewer and water heater:** these are urgent sub-niches with rankability 5 (vs 3 for general plumbing) [PRIOR] and a plumbing offer snippet behind them (E-11).

## Pre-mortem: "it's 12 months later and it failed because..."
1. **No network ever paid for organic calls.** The operator was rejected as a new publisher, or approved but capped and outside the target ZIPs. The site ranked for some terms, but calls went to an offer that rejected them as "not qualified" or "outside hours". (O-1, O-6)
2. **The site ranked on page 1, but nobody saw it.** LSAs, 3 ads, the map pack and an AI Overview took the emergency clicks for water damage and HVAC. The site picked up 1-3 leads a month, below the 8 assumed. (O-4, O-5)
3. **The operator ran out of hours.** With no budget for content or links, the content plan stalled around month 3, and the brief's month-6 ranking milestone was missed. (O-9)

## Opportunity cost
In the team's own data, **foundation repair** (rankability 6, risk 9.4, no LSA flag, payout prior $40-100) and **basement waterproofing** (rankability 6, payout prior $40-100, observed $25-70 snippet E-12) have better rankability than HVAC or general plumbing, and comparable payout priors to the trades. Neither memo puts them forward as a candidate. The economist names foundation repair only as an alternate. I don't claim that either is better, only that HVAC's place on the list is less defended than foundation repair's would be. **My recommended advance set, subject to O-1:** tree service and water damage (with conditions O-2 and O-5), plus drain/sewer as the trade sub-niche. Septic advances as a tenant-first fallback only. Pest control and HVAC do not advance unless their offer and SERP evidence beats the others.
