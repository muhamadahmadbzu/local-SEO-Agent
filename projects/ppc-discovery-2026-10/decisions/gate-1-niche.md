## Decision in one paragraph
Three niches go on to $0 research: **tree service, water damage and drain/sewer**. **Septic** is kept as a tenant-first fallback only and is not on the PPC track. Pest control and HVAC are dropped. Gate 1 approves $0 research only. It does **not** approve buying a domain or spending any money. The score is at the bottom of the CONDITIONAL band (55) for three reasons. Nothing in the record is MEASURED. Every payout is OBSERVED-snippet or PRIOR. And O-1 is still open: nobody has shown that a zero-history SEO publisher can get approved for any offer. Both owners mitigated O-1 with a $0, 14-day network-application test, but neither resolved it. The biggest risk we are accepting is that this test fails. If it does, the brief's PPC-first mission fails for every niche, and the project becomes a different, tenant-first project.

## Kill criteria (measurable, dated)
1. 0 network approvals that allow SEO traffic in an inland target market by **2026-10-17** -> stop the PPC-first track. Re-scope as tenant-first or direct per-call (septic or tree to lead). Contacting businesses needs operator approval.
2. A niche gets no approved offer that allows SEO and covers the candidate ZIPs by 2026-10-17 -> that niche is dropped. Foundation repair is the alternate.
3. Organic #1 sits below LSA + 3 ads + pack + AIO on 2 of 3 money queries in both cities (SERP check due 2026-10-24) -> that niche is dropped, or limited to a long-tail cost/guide play only.
4. Measured core volume cannot support about 8 leads/mo in any candidate city (KWP due 2026-10-24) -> that niche does not reach Gate 3.
5. Conditions 3 and 4 are not complete by 2026-10-24 -> Gate 2 does not convene. If they are still missing at Gate 3, it is an automatic NO-GO.

## Dissent on record
Red team (O-1, pre-mortem #1): until any network approves a zero-history SEO publisher, ranking niches is meaningless, and the honest verdict is "no niche selected yet". I rule against a NO-GO because Gate 1 commits $0 and the test that settles the question is free and due in 14 days. The dissent is kept as kill criterion 1.
Second dissent (scout's own rubric): septic scores highest on the rubric (3.85) and has the best rankability (9). It may beat all three PPC niches if the project turns tenant-first.
